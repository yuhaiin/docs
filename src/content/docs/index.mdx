## Get connected

Begin with [installation](/documents/guides/install/) and [your first connection](/documents/guides/first-connection/). No networking experience is required: the guides explain what each setting does and how to verify it worked.

## Configure your traffic

Use [Nodes and subscriptions](/documents/features/nodes/) to choose an outgoing connection, [Inbounds](/documents/features/inbounds/) to receive app traffic, and [Routing rules](/documents/features/routing/) to decide where it goes.

## Find the problem

The [Troubleshooting guide](/documents/guides/troubleshooting/) starts with simple checks. [Network diagnostics](/documents/features/diagnostics/) separates DNS, routing and outbound failures.

## Dig deeper

Read the [Protocol reference](/documents/protocol/) or [Configuration model](/documents/reference/configuration/) when you need to work with the backend directly. The [glossary](/documents/guides/glossary/) defines unfamiliar terms.
